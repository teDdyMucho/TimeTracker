## 1. Why payroll and the timesheet disagreed
The timesheet PDF read `clock_sessions`; payroll read `timesheets`. They were meant to agree but didn't:
- **Add Attendance created a session but never a timesheet** — 7 days (~65h) existed on the PDF and were invisible to payroll.
- **Corrected session times left stale timesheets** — William Kong: an 8.10h session against a 0.02h timesheet.
- **The old 16h auto clock-out wrote a timesheet unrelated to any session** — Mitchell 2 Oct: 8.83h worked, 18.83h on payroll.

No timesheet existed without a clock-in, which confirmed sessions are the complete record.

## 2. Timesheets are now generated from clock sessions ✅
`0024` + `0025`: a closed session owns exactly one timesheet, kept in step by trigger however the session was created or later edited. Rejected attendance removes it; an open session has none; a locked timesheet in a finished pay run is never moved.
- Verified 8/8 against the live database (create, pending, rejected, open, approval, time correction, rejection, session delete).
- Backfill rebuilt all 129 old rows → **148 timesheets, every one linked to a session, zero unlinked**.
- One gotcha cost time: the unique index is partial, so `ON CONFLICT` had to repeat its `WHERE` clause; without it every insert failed silently inside the function's exception handler.

## 3. Approved attendance only ✅
Client rule. Payroll, project cost totals, the Xero preview, the Xero push **and the timesheet PDF** all took `submitted` too, so unreviewed hours were shown and paid. All five now require `approved`.
- Verified: **138 approved sessions → 138 correct approved timesheets**, zero missing, zero wrong status, zero wrong hours, zero orphans.

## 4. Deactivated staff excluded from payroll ✅
Nothing filtered on profile status, so a deactivated worker's old approved hours were still paid — and Mitch Baldry's archived duplicate was paid alongside his active account. Payroll, project totals, Xero preview and Xero push now require `status = 'active'`.
- Verified: 25 Sep–8 Oct unchanged; 11–24 Sep drops 223.65h → 80.78h as the two archived duplicates fall away.
