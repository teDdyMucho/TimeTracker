## 1. Morning clock-in reminders ✅ (v7.2)
- Two alerts in the **phone's local time**, wording verbatim from the client:
  - 6:50 — "Morning team, 10 minutes to go" / "Clock in when you arrive so your hours are recorded correctly."
  - 7:10 — "It's 7:10am, just checking you haven't forgotten to clock in" / "Clock in straight away, or message the office if you're late or away."
- Local notifications (`expo-notifications`), so they fire with no signal and follow DST automatically.
- Scheduled as one-offs for the next 14 days, topped up every app open — so **clocking in cancels that day's remaining alerts** (no "have you forgotten?" at 7:10 for someone who clocked in at 6:58). Cancelled on sign-out and when notifications are switched off in Settings.
- **Every day incl. weekends**, as literally requested. One constant (`CLOCK_IN_REMINDER_DAYS`) switches it to Mon–Fri.
- Caveat: a worker who doesn't open the app for 14 days stops receiving them until they do.

## 2. Bugs found — the two existing reminders never worked
- `scheduleClockOutReminder` (8h "Time to clock out") and `scheduleDailyTimesheetReminder` (5pm "Log your hours") passed **untyped** triggers. Proven against the real library: it **rejects** them; the error was swallowed by `try/catch`, so neither reminder was ever scheduled. Both now use typed `DATE` / `DAILY` triggers. (Earlier in the day I described this as "fired immediately" — the real-library test corrected that: they never scheduled at all.)
- **Android exact alarms:** without `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, expo-notifications falls back to an inexact alarm on Android 12+, which can delay the 6:50 alert. Declared the permission (per Expo's own changelog). On **Android 14+** it is off by default — the worker must enable "Alarms & reminders" for Timevera in phone settings, or alerts may run a few minutes late.
