## 1. Research — confirmed the push approach (Xero Payroll AU) ✅
- **Timesheets:** POST an APPROVED timesheet and Xero auto-populates the matching draft pay run's payslips. We do **not** need to create pay runs or payslips ourselves. Scope: `payroll.timesheets`. Requirement: earnings rates must be RatePerUnit and on each employee's pay template; timesheet period must align to the pay calendar.
- **Leave:** POST a leave application (5 fields) and Xero auto-computes the units and flows it onto the payslip. Scope: `payroll.employees` (write). Map our leave types to Xero LeaveTypes via STP category codes (ANNUALLEAVE, PERSONALSICKCARERSLEAVE) / name.

## 2. Admin — "Push to Xero" feature ✅ (built)
- One **"Push to Xero"** button per pay run (Payroll page). Confirms first, then pushes and shows a per-employee result modal.
- **Pushes worked hours** as APPROVED timesheets — matches each worker to their Xero employee (by email/name) and each pay band (Ordinary, OT 1.5×, OT 2×, Saturday, Sunday, Public Holiday) to the right Xero earnings rate by name.
- **Pushes approved leave** as leave applications — pulls approved `leave_requests` overlapping the period and maps Annual/Sick/Personal/Unpaid to the Xero leave type.
- Result modal shows two sections: **Timesheets (n/n)** and **Leave (n/n)**, with skipped/unmapped reasons per employee.
- Files: `lib/xero.ts` (postXeroTimesheet, postXeroLeave, fetchXeroLeaveTypes, matchEarningsRateId, matchLeaveTypeId), `api/xero/push-timesheet/route.ts` (does both hours + leave), `api/xero/push-leave/route.ts`, `payroll/push-to-xero.tsx`.
